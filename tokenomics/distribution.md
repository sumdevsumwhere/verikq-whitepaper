# Token Distribution

## Allocation Breakdown

The total $VQ supply is distributed across five categories, designed to balance ecosystem growth, team incentives, liquidity, research, and community engagement.

```
         │         $VQ Distribution          │

         │  ███████▌            15%  R&D                │
         │  ██▌                  5%  Airdrops           │
         └──────────────────────────────────┘
```

## Detailed Allocation

### Ecosystem — 40%

The largest allocation, dedicated to fueling platform growth and adoption.

- **Staking rewards** for validators and verifiers
- **Compute provider incentives** to bootstrap the marketplace
- **Developer grants** for building on VeriQ infrastructure
- **Partnership integrations** with blockchain protocols
- **Vesting:** Released over 48 months with quarterly unlocks

### Team — 20%

Reserved for the founding team and future contributors.

- **Purpose:** Align team incentives with long-term platform success
- **Vesting:** 12-month cliff, then linear vesting over 36 months
- **Lock:** Tokens are non-transferable during the cliff period

### Liquidity — 20%

Ensures healthy trading conditions and market stability.

- **DEX liquidity pools** on PancakeSwap and other BSC DEXs
- **CEX listings** as the project scales
- **Market-making partnerships** for price stability
- **Initial allocation:** Deployed at TGE (Token Generation Event)

### Research & Development — 15%

Funds ongoing technical development and quantum computing research.

- **Quantum algorithm research** and optimization
- **Security audits** of VeriQ's own smart contracts
- **Academic partnerships** with quantum computing institutions
- **Open-source contributions** to the broader quantum-blockchain space
- **Vesting:** Released over 36 months based on milestone completion

### Airdrops — 5%

Community building through strategic token distribution.

- **Early adopter rewards** for platform beta testers
- **Community campaigns** to drive awareness and engagement
- **Bug bounty rewards** for security researchers
- **Strategic airdrops** to BNB Smart Chain ecosystem participants
