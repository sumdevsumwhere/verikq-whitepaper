# Roadmap

## Development Timeline

VeriQ's roadmap is structured in phases, each building on the previous to deliver a comprehensive quantum security platform.

---

### Q4 2025 — Foundation

> Platform launch and ecosystem bootstrapping

- Launch of **VeriQ dApp** on BNB Smart Chain
- Governance token **$VQ** distribution begins
- Initial liquidity deployment on PancakeSwap
- Community building and early adopter onboarding
- Smart contract deployment and initial audit

**Milestone:** Platform live with basic audit functionality

---

### Q1 2026 — Core Product

> Minimum viable audit system goes live

- **MVP of on-chain audit system** — users can submit quantum vulnerability assessments
- Integration with quantum simulation frameworks (Qiskit, Cirq)
- Beta testing program with selected blockchain projects
- First round of staking rewards activated

**Milestone:** First on-chain quantum audit completed

---

### Q2 2026 — Marketplace Launch

> Decentralized compute marketplace opens

- Launch of **quantum compute marketplace**
- Provider onboarding and incentive program
- Escrow and verification smart contracts deployed
- SDK release for third-party integrations

**Milestone:** First marketplace transaction completed

---

### Q3 2026 — Hardware Integration

> Bridging simulation to real quantum hardware

- Integration with **quantum hardware providers** (IBM Quantum, Amazon Braket, etc.)
- Hybrid classical-quantum execution pipeline
- Enhanced verification protocols for hardware results
- Performance benchmarking and optimization

**Milestone:** First audit executed on real quantum hardware

---

### Q4 2026 — Decentralization

> Full community governance and mainnet maturity

- **Mainnet launch** with decentralized governance
- Governance multisig transitions to full DAO control
- Cross-chain bridge deployment (Ethereum, Solana compatibility)
- Enterprise partnership program launch

**Milestone:** Fully decentralized governance operational

---

### 2027+ — Expansion

> Advanced capabilities and ecosystem growth

- **Advanced quantum DeFi modules** — quantum-optimized yield strategies, risk assessment
- **Post-quantum cryptographic integrations** — lattice-based, hash-based, and code-based schemes
- Multi-chain deployment across major Layer 1 and Layer 2 networks
- Quantum machine learning modules for predictive security analysis
- Academic research publication and open-source framework release

**Milestone:** VeriQ becomes the standard for quantum blockchain security
