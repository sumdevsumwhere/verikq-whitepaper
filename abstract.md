# Abstract

This document outlines the risks posed by quantum computing to blockchain systems and introduces **VeriQ** as a solution.

The platform combines:

- **Quantum simulation** — modeling quantum attacks on classical cryptographic systems
- **Decentralized compute** — distributing quantum workloads across a trustless network
- **Blockchain-based verification** — ensuring result integrity through on-chain cryptographic proofs

Together, these components deliver **scalable security audits and optimization tools** that prepare blockchain infrastructure for the post-quantum era.

## Scope

This whitepaper covers the following areas:

1. The quantum threat to blockchain cryptography
2. VeriQ's platform architecture and technical approach
3. Tokenomics and incentive design
4. Roadmap and strategic positioning
5. Risk analysis and mitigation strategies

## Target Audience

This document is intended for:

- **Blockchain developers** seeking to understand quantum risks to their protocols
- **Security researchers** interested in quantum attack simulation
- **Investors and stakeholders** evaluating the VeriQ ecosystem
- **Node operators and validators** looking to participate in decentralized quantum compute
